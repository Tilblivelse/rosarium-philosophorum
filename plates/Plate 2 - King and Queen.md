# Plate 2: King and Queen

## Image
A king and a queen approach each other. Each stands on a celestial body: the king on the sun and the queen on the moon. They wear court dress and touch left hands, then right hands. The right hands hold a device of five flowers: four branches bearing two flowers each, and a fifth flower brought from above by a dove descending from a star.

## Provenance of this description
The description above is paraphrased from the ARAS concordance of Jung's Psychology of the Transference ([ARAS concordance, Rosarium 2](https://aras.org/concordance/rosarium-2-king-and-queen)). It has not been checked against the 1550 print. The title is the conventional title used in that source, not a caption from the print.
