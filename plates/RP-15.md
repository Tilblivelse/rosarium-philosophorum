# Plate 15: Falling Dew on the Hermaphrodite

## Image
Identical to Plate 8: the hermaphrodite in the sarcophagus with heavenly dew falling on it.

## Text and motto
Henderson cites Plate 8's text: 'Whiten the lato and rend the books lest your hearts be rent asunder.'

## Alchemical stage
End of nigredo and start of albedo (Plate 8)

## Reading (Henderson, second series)
Joseph L. Henderson, presentation of 18 March 1990 on the second half of the series, summarised by Daniel S. Benveniste (ARAS Connections 2023, Issue 2).

- Henderson: intellectual understanding is inappropriate here; the process calls for feeling rather than thinking, and trying to think one's way through misses its most important element.

## Jung
Jung's Psychology of the Transference (1946) comments on plates 1-10 only.

## Other witnesses and notes
- Compare Plate 8.
- Glasgow article's numbering is inconsistent around this plate: its caption 15 reads 'the soul child born of the conjunction between Sun and Moon', with a detail of the Sun Tree, while the image files for those are labelled 17.
- Adam McLean, commentary in the Magnum Opus edition of the Rosary of the Philosophers (Edinburgh, 1980), hosted on alchemywebsite.com. Layer 3, one author's view. McLean reads all twenty woodcuts as one pattern (Body, Soul and Spirit) and presents his reading as one interpretation among others. What he says about this plate specifically has not yet been read.
