# Plate 10: The New Birth (Apotheosis of the Rebis)

## Image
A winged figure, male on its right side and female on its left, stands on the moon. In one hand it holds a chalice with three snakes (or one three-headed snake) and in the other a single snake. At the left is a raven; at the right is a tree bearing the sun and the moon.

## Provenance of this description
The description above is paraphrased from the ARAS concordance of Jung's Psychology of the Transference ([ARAS concordance, Rosarium 10](https://aras.org/concordance/rosarium-10-new-birth)). It has not been checked against the 1550 print. The title is the conventional title used in that source, not a caption from the print.
