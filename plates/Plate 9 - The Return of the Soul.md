# Plate 9: The Return of the Soul

## Image
![Plate 9](https://www.alchemywebsite.com/virtual_museum/Images/RS09.jpg)

Image link: [RS09.jpg](https://www.alchemywebsite.com/virtual_museum/Images/RS09.jpg) (hosted by alchemywebsite.com; no licence stated)

A soul figure descends from above toward a dead body. Below is a hermaphrodite figure that is described as united and about to come alive. In the bottom left corner are two birds, one winged and one wingless. ARAS gives no further detail on colours, sizes or the soul's exact placement.

Source of this description: [ARAS concordance](https://aras.org/concordance/rosarium-9-return-soul). Inscriptions are as reported by ARAS in English and have not been checked against the print.

## Provenance of this description
The description above is paraphrased from the ARAS concordance of Jung's Psychology of the Transference ([ARAS concordance, Rosarium 9](https://aras.org/concordance/rosarium-9-return-soul)). It has not been checked against the 1550 print. The title is the conventional title used in that source, not a caption from the print.

The English text of the whole work is on [alchemywebsite.com](https://alchemywebsite.com/rosary0.html), described there as a transcription of the English translation in MS Ferguson 210. It is a later English translation, not the 1550 Latin and German. The page names no translator or editor and states no rights; booksellers list Adam McLean's Magnum Opus Hermetic Sourceworks No. 6 as an edited English translation from that manuscript.

## Documentary notes
Each item is attributed to its source and describes a witness or a fact about the edition, not a reading of the picture. Glasgow's captions describe the 18th-century MS Ferguson 210 copy, not the 1550 print.

- Glasgow MS Ferguson 210, illustration 9: titled "Of the rejoicing or springing or sublimation of the soul", with two birds meeting above and below ground ([Glasgow, MS Ferguson 210](https://www.gla.ac.uk/myglasgow/library/files/special/exhibns/month/april2009.html)). The same page describes the soul's return to the body again under illustration 12 and does not clearly separate the two.
