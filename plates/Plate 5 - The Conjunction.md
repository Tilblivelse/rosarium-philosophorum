# Plate 5: The Conjunction

## Image
![Plate 5](https://www.alchemywebsite.com/virtual_museum/Images/RS05.jpg)

Image link: [RS05.jpg](https://www.alchemywebsite.com/virtual_museum/Images/RS05.jpg) (hosted by alchemywebsite.com; no licence stated)

The king and queen (Sol and Luna) lie embracing in the water of a sea that has closed over them. ARAS prints two verses with the figure and does not say whether they stand inside the woodcut or beneath it. A variant, ARAS Figure 5a, shows Sol and Luna in the water again, but with both figures winged.

Source of this description: [ARAS concordance](https://aras.org/concordance/rosarium-5-conjunction). Inscriptions are as reported by ARAS in English and have not been checked against the print.

## Provenance of this description
The description above is paraphrased from the ARAS concordance of Jung's Psychology of the Transference ([ARAS concordance, Rosarium 5](https://aras.org/concordance/rosarium-5-conjunction)). It has not been checked against the 1550 print. The title is the conventional title used in that source, not a caption from the print.

The English text of the whole work is on [alchemywebsite.com](https://alchemywebsite.com/rosary0.html), described there as a transcription of the English translation in MS Ferguson 210. It is a later English translation, not the 1550 Latin and German. The page names no translator or editor and states no rights; booksellers list Adam McLean's Magnum Opus Hermetic Sourceworks No. 6 as an edited English translation from that manuscript.

## Documentary notes
Each item is attributed to its source and describes a witness or a fact about the edition, not a reading of the picture. Glasgow's captions describe the 18th-century MS Ferguson 210 copy, not the 1550 print.

- Glasgow MS Ferguson 210, illustration 5: captioned "Conception or Putrefaction" ([Glasgow, MS Ferguson 210](https://www.gla.ac.uk/myglasgow/library/files/special/exhibns/month/april2009.html)). ARAS gives "Conceptio" as an alternative title for Plate 6 instead, so the two sources attach the title to different pictures.
- ARAS records a variant of this picture, Figure 5a, in which both figures are winged; the winged form also appears as Plate 11 in the second series.
