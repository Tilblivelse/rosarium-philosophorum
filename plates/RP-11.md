# Plate 11: Sol and Luna in the Waters (Fermentation)

## Image
Sol and Luna copulate in the waters. Luna is on top; both are winged.

## Text and motto
No verse or motto cited in the sources consulted.

## Alchemical stage
Coniunctio (Henderson links the image to the coniunctio); Glasgow caption 'Fermentation'

## Reading (Henderson, second series)
Joseph L. Henderson, presentation of 18 March 1990 on the second half of the series, summarised by Daniel S. Benveniste (ARAS Connections 2023, Issue 2).

- Henderson: parallels the frequent emergence of an incestuous undercurrent or erotic transference in analysis ('The incestuous undercurrent is an important theme throughout Jung's treatment of the transference').

## Jung
Jung's Psychology of the Transference (1946) comments on plates 1-10 only.

## Other witnesses and notes
- Glasgow: caption 'Fermentation'; opens the second cycle (plates 11-17), dealing with mastery of the solar element or Red Stone; images 5 and 11 both show the conjunction. A version of this image was used as cover art for Leonard Cohen's 'New Skin for Old Ceremony' (a wing moved to cover the figures' abdominal areas; later withdrawn).
- Adam McLean, commentary in the Magnum Opus edition of the Rosary of the Philosophers (Edinburgh, 1980), hosted on alchemywebsite.com. Layer 3, one author's view. McLean reads all twenty woodcuts as one pattern (Body, Soul and Spirit) and presents his reading as one interpretation among others. What he says about this plate specifically has not yet been read.
