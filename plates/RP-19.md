# Plate 19: The Crowning of the Virgin

## Image
The Father and the Son crown a young woman kneeling between them, with a dove (the Holy Ghost) above her head.

## Text and motto
No verse or motto cited in the sources consulted.

## Alchemical stage
Not named; culmination of the series (Glasgow: 'Crowning of Nature')

## Reading (Henderson, second series)
Joseph L. Henderson, presentation of 18 March 1990 on the second half of the series, summarised by Daniel S. Benveniste (ARAS Connections 2023, Issue 2).

- Henderson: 'elevation of the feminine into the masculine world'.

## Jung
Jung's Psychology of the Transference (1946) comments on plates 1-10 only.

## Other witnesses and notes
- Wikipedia: woodcuts 10, 17 and 19 have precedents in the Buch der heiligen Dreifaltigkeit (c. 1410).
- Glasgow: the Coronation of the Virgin Mary by the Holy Trinity, the 'Crowning of Nature'; plates 19 and 20 conflate Christian and alchemical imagery. Wikipedia notes Plate 19 has a precedent in the Buch der heiligen Dreifaltigkeit (c. 1410).
- Adam McLean, commentary in the Magnum Opus edition of the Rosary of the Philosophers (Edinburgh, 1980), hosted on alchemywebsite.com. Layer 3, one author's view. McLean reads all twenty woodcuts as one pattern (Body, Soul and Spirit) and presents his reading as one interpretation among others. What he says about this plate specifically has not yet been read.
