# Plate 16: The Female Soul Descends

## Image
As Plate 9, the return of the soul to the hermaphrodite's body, but the descending soul figure is female.

## Text and motto
No verse or motto cited in the sources consulted.

## Alchemical stage
Transition out of the nigredo (by comparison with Plate 9); not named directly

## Reading (Henderson, second series)
Joseph L. Henderson, presentation of 18 March 1990 on the second half of the series, summarised by Daniel S. Benveniste (ARAS Connections 2023, Issue 2).

- No interpretation recorded in the Henderson summary.

## Jung
Jung's Psychology of the Transference (1946) comments on plates 1-10 only.
