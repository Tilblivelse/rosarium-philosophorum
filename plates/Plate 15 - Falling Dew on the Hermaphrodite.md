# Plate 15: Falling Dew on the Hermaphrodite

## Image
Identical to Plate 8: the hermaphrodite in the sarcophagus with heavenly dew falling on it.

## Text and motto
Henderson cites Plate 8's text: 'Whiten the lato and rend the books lest your hearts be rent asunder.'

## Alchemical stage
End of nigredo and start of albedo (Plate 8)

### Etymology and symbolism
- Albedo: a Latinised term meaning 'whiteness'; Wikipedia describes the stage as ablutio, the washing away of impurities, following the nigredo ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albedo_%28alchemy%29)). This page records the stage of this plate as the end of the nigredo and the start of the albedo, by comparison with Plate 8, and Henderson quotes its text 'Whiten the lato and rend the books'.
- Etymologies are from the sources linked here. Where a plate source (ARAS, Glasgow, Henderson) uses the term, the link to the plate is that source's; whether any source draws a symbolic connection from the word's origin itself has not been checked.

## Reading (Henderson, second series)
Joseph L. Henderson, presentation of 18 March 1990 on the second half of the series, summarised by Daniel S. Benveniste (ARAS Connections 2023, Issue 2).

- Henderson: intellectual understanding is inappropriate here; the process calls for feeling rather than thinking, and trying to think one's way through misses its most important element.

## Jung
Jung's Psychology of the Transference (1946) comments on plates 1-10 only.

## Other witnesses and notes
- Compare Plate 8.
- Glasgow article's numbering is inconsistent around this plate: its caption 15 reads 'the soul child born of the conjunction between Sun and Moon', with a detail of the Sun Tree, while the image files for those are labelled 17.
- Adam McLean, commentary in the Magnum Opus edition of the Rosary of the Philosophers (Edinburgh, 1980), hosted on alchemywebsite.com. Layer 3, one author's view. McLean reads all twenty woodcuts as one pattern (Body, Soul and Spirit) and presents his reading as one interpretation among others. What he says about this plate specifically has not yet been read. Full entry: [[Plate 1 - The Mercurial Fountain|Plate 1: The Mercurial Fountain]].
- Glasgow inconsistencies around 15 and 17 (as reported on the exhibition page): (1) the 'Sun Tree' detail is labelled as from illustration 15, but the Sun Tree appears in the caption of illustration 17; (2) the caption 'demonstration of perfection' is attached to the image captioned 15, yet illustration 17 carries the same title; (3) the image captioned 15 is filed as '17_Hermaphrodite_with_title' and its detail as '17_detail_suntree'; (4) the caption describes a 'soul child' born of the Sun-Moon conjunction while the text describes a King and Queen in the chemical wedding. See [[Plate 17 - The Demonstration of Perfection (Bat-winged Hermaphrodite)|Plate 17: The Demonstration of Perfection (Bat-winged Hermaphrodite)]].
