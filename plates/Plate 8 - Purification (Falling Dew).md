# Plate 8: Purification (Falling Dew)

## Image
![Plate 8](https://www.alchemywebsite.com/virtual_museum/Images/RS08.jpg)

Image link: [RS08.jpg](https://www.alchemywebsite.com/virtual_museum/Images/RS08.jpg) (hosted by alchemywebsite.com; no licence stated)

ARAS gives only the caption verse and commentary for this picture, not a description of the woodcut. The verse speaks of heavenly dew falling on a dark, soiled body in a grave. No other figures, objects or inscriptions are named. A direct description remains to be entered from the print or a facsimile.

Source of this description: [ARAS concordance](https://aras.org/concordance/rosarium-8-purification). Inscriptions are as reported by ARAS in English and have not been checked against the print.

## Provenance of this description
The description above is paraphrased from the ARAS concordance of Jung's Psychology of the Transference ([ARAS concordance, Rosarium 8](https://aras.org/concordance/rosarium-8-purification)). It has not been checked against the 1550 print. The title is the conventional title used in that source, not a caption from the print.

The English text of the whole work is on [alchemywebsite.com](https://alchemywebsite.com/rosary0.html), described there as a transcription of the English translation in MS Ferguson 210. It is a later English translation, not the 1550 Latin and German. The page names no translator or editor and states no rights; booksellers list Adam McLean's Magnum Opus Hermetic Sourceworks No. 6 as an edited English translation from that manuscript.
