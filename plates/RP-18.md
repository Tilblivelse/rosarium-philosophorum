# Plate 18: The Green Lion Devours the Sun

## Image
A green lion sits on the ground, head raised, eating the sun.

## Text and motto
No verse or motto cited in the sources consulted.

## Alchemical stage
Not named

## Reading (Henderson, second series)
Joseph L. Henderson, presentation of 18 March 1990 on the second half of the series, summarised by Daniel S. Benveniste (ARAS Connections 2023, Issue 2).

- Henderson: the green lion is nature and the sun is spirit: 'nature is eating the spirit, and that is what might happen if the unconscious were activated too completely'. He urges the alchemists' attitude of 'patience in enduring the whole process as it unfolds'.

## Jung
Jung's Psychology of the Transference (1946) comments on plates 1-10 only.

## Other witnesses and notes
- Glasgow: a classic alchemical symbol; the gold or sun is dissolved or purified in a powerful solvent so that the seed from which pure gold may be grown is released; the images have many possible meanings.
