# Plate 13: The Winged Hermaphrodite in the Sarcophagus

## Image
The winged hermaphrodite lies in the sarcophagus.

## Text and motto
No verse or motto cited in the sources consulted.

## Alchemical stage
Not named; resembles Plate 6 (nigredo)

### Etymology and symbolism
- Sarcophagus: Greek sarkophagos, 'flesh-eating' limestone used for coffins ([Etymonline](https://www.etymonline.com/word/sarcophagus)). The winged hermaphrodite lies in the sarcophagus on this plate.
- Nigredo: Henderson compares this plate to Plate 6, the dead hermaphrodite of the nigredo; for the term see the Plate 6 page ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigredo)).
- Etymologies are from the sources linked here. Where a plate source (ARAS, Glasgow, Henderson) uses the term, the link to the plate is that source's; whether any source draws a symbolic connection from the word's origin itself has not been checked.
