## Method
- Plates 1-10: pages follow the ARAS concordance of Jung's Psychology of the Transference (section lettering and CW16 paragraph numbers are ARAS's).
- Plates 11-20: Joseph Henderson's 1990 presentation as summarised by Benveniste (ARAS Connections 2023, issue 2); Jung did not comment on these.
- Glasgow MS Ferguson 210 (an 18th-century English copy, altered from the 1550 woodcuts) is cited as a secondary witness where it gives captions.
- Verse lines are in the English renderings quoted by ARAS. The 1550 original wording is not yet entered.
- Descriptions are paraphrased from those sources, not taken from the print directly. Every page is marked verified_against_1550_print: false.
