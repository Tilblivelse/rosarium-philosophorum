## Sources

- ARAS concordance of Jung's Psychology of the Transference: basis for the plate 1-10 pages, including section lettering and CW16 paragraph numbers, which are ARAS's.
- ARAS, On the Rosarium Philosophorum (Benveniste, with Henderson): basis for the plate 11-20 pages. It cites Johannes Fabricius, Alchemy (1976), and Jung, The Practice of Psychotherapy (1954); those passages have not yet been read.
- Glasgow University Library, MS Ferguson 210 (English, 18th century, twenty coloured miniatures altered from the printed woodcuts): cited as a secondary witness where its page gives captions. Its numbering is inconsistent around plates 15 and 17, which is recorded on both pages. Other Glasgow Ferguson manuscripts (6, 29, 74, 96, 149) are listed on the plate 1 page.
- Wikipedia, Rosary of the Philosophers: edition details, printed reprises, manuscript list, translations and the Buch der heiligen Dreifaltigkeit precedents for woodcuts 10, 17 and 19.
- McLean's commentary on alchemywebsite.com, which also hosts the images linked from each page and a transcription of the MS Ferguson 210 translation.
